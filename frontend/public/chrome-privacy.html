Cubie for Chrome privacy policy

Cubie by RubikSoft. Effective October 28, 2026.

Cubie for Chrome is a Chrome extension for people whose company uses Cubie, RubikSoft's work app. It shows a person their own overdue commitments and today's priorities from Cubie on Chrome's new tab page, with links back to Cubie. It is published privately, and only people with an account at a company that uses Cubie can install it.

What it handles

  • A connection key. When a person connects the extension from Cubie, Cubie creates a random key for that browser. The extension keeps it in Chrome's extension storage on that computer and sends it only to Cubie. Cubie stores only a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself.
  • The person's own work items and first name, as Cubie sends them: their overdue or due-today to-dos and commitments, and the priorities Cubie prepared for them that morning. The extension keeps the latest copy in the same storage so a new tab opens instantly.
  • Display preferences (how much the new tab shows, whether the toolbar number is on). These are kept in Chrome's synced storage for that person.

What it does not do

  • It does not read the person's email, browsing history or the content of other websites.
  • It has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and sends nothing to any third party.
  • Its data is not sold, not shared, and not used for any purpose other than showing the person their own work.
  • It talks to one address only: the Cubie address of the person's company.

Keeping and removing data

A connection key stops working after 90 days, when the person disconnects the browser (in the extension's options or in Cubie under Settings → General), or when the person leaves the company. Disconnecting also clears the copy of their items from the browser. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on that computer.
