Cubie for Chrome

Your promises and today's priorities from Cubie, on every new tab.

Connection

New tab

"Hide for today" at the bottom of the new tab hides Cubie until tomorrow.

Toolbar

Shortcuts

  • Type cubie and a space in the address bar, then your question, to ask Cubie.
  • Select text on any page, right-click, and choose Ask Cubie about….

What this extension can see

Only what Cubie sends it: your promises past due and this morning's priorities. It does not read your email, your browsing or any other website. It never asks Cubie to make new priorities, so opening tabs costs nothing.
